Some Apps add features such as notifications, crash reporting, in-app purchases or sign-in. We do not sell your personal information. Questions? Email shani.ktk77@gmail.com.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information our apps, games and other digital services may collect or process, why, who it may be shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. We have tried to write it in plain language. Where a section applies only to some kinds of App, it says so.

By using an App you acknowledge the practices described here. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the App, or uninstall it.

2. Who we are

Sk_Tech is an independent developer of mobile apps and games. Sk_Tech is responsible for the Apps covered by this policy. You can reach us at shani.ktk77@gmail.com; full contact details are in section 28.

3. Scope and applicability

This one policy applies to every app, game, software product and digital service that Sk_Tech publishes or operates, now and in the future, wherever it is distributed (for example on Google Play or, where applicable, Apple's App Store), and that links to this page from its store listing or from within the product (each an "App"). That includes, for example, games, health and wellness apps, productivity, utility, entertainment and educational apps.

Not every App uses every service or feature described here. A specific App may use some, all or none of them, depending on how it is built. The fact that this policy describes a kind of information does not mean every App collects it, and information collected by one App is not automatically collected by, or combined with, our other Apps. Where an App's store listing, data safety section or in-app notice gives more specific information about that App, that information applies in addition to this policy.

This policy does not cover the websites, apps or services of third parties, including advertisers whose ads appear in our Apps. Their own privacy policies apply when you interact with them.

The information an App processes falls into the categories described in sections 5 to 14. 5. Information you choose to give us

Many of our Apps, including our games, do not require you to create an account or to enter your name, email address or phone number. Some Apps may let you enter information yourself, for example notes, records or preferences; that information is used to provide the feature you are using and is handled as described in sections 10 and 11.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include in your message, and we use it to respond to you. If an App offers sign-in, see section 22.

6. Device and technical information

When an App uses the third-party services described in sections 12 to 14, those services may automatically collect or process:

  • Device and app identifiers, such as the device's advertising ID (for example, the Android advertising ID), app set ID, Firebase installation ID and app instance ID.
  • Device and technical information, such as device model, operating system and version, language and region settings, screen size, app version, and network or carrier information.
  • IP address, which is transmitted whenever a device connects to these services.
  • Approximate location, which may be inferred from your IP address. This is not the same as precise location; see section 8.

7. App usage information

  • Usage and gameplay information, such as when the App is opened, sessions and their length, screens or levels visited, and in-app events (for example levels started and completed, features used and ads shown).
  • Advertising information, such as ads shown, ad clicks and other interactions with ads.
  • Diagnostic and crash information, such as crash logs, stack traces, device state at the time of a crash (for example, available memory and disk space, orientation and whether the device is rooted) and performance data.

Apps that include one of the optional features in section 14 may process a few additional items, such as a notification token, purchase confirmations or a player ID.

8. Sensitive and health-related information

Some kinds of information need extra care: health and wellness information, financial information and precise location. Most of our Apps, including our games, do not ask for any of it. Where an App does, the rules below apply.

Health and wellness information

A health or wellness App may let you record information such as symptoms, measurements, moods, habits or other personal tracking records.

  • Why: only to provide the features you use, such as showing your records, history or summaries.
  • Where it is stored: on your device, unless the App clearly tells you, before you use that feature, that it is stored or backed up elsewhere and where (see section 11).
  • Whether it is transmitted: information kept on your device is not sent to us. We do not send it to advertising or analytics providers.
  • Who can access it: you, through the App on your device. We do not have access to information that stays on your device.
  • How it is protected: see sections 20 and 21.
  • How to delete it: see section 18.

We do not use health or wellness information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to decide which ads you see. Ads shown in an App, if any, are handled as described in section 12, separately from any health information you record. Our Apps are not medical devices and are not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Financial information

Purchases inside our Apps are handled by the app store (see section 14). We do not receive or store your card or payment details.

Precise location

Our games do not request access to your device's precise (GPS) location. If any App needs precise location for a feature, it will ask for your permission first through your device's permission prompt, explain why, and use it only for that feature. You can refuse or revoke that permission at any time.

9. Permissions and device access

Some Apps may ask for access to parts of your device, such as notifications, the camera, files you select, or location. An App asks only for permissions it needs for its features, and uses each one only for the feature it was requested for.

  • Where your device's operating system requires it, you are asked before access is granted.
  • You can refuse a permission, or revoke it later in your device settings. Some features may not work without it.
  • Files or other content you choose to open in an App are processed on your device to provide the feature, unless the App tells you otherwise.

Some services, such as AdMob and Firebase, use standard access that does not require a prompt, such as internet access and access to the advertising ID.

10. Information stored on your device

Our Apps store information locally on your device so they can pick up where you left off: for example, levels completed, sound or language options, preferences, and in some Apps the records or content you create. This information stays on your device unless the App includes a sign-in, backup or synchronization feature (see sections 11 and 22). You can remove it by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. Your device's own backup settings (for example, Android backup to your Google account) may also back up app data; that is controlled by your device settings, not by us.

11. Cloud storage and synchronization

Most of our Apps do not store your information in the cloud. Some may: for example, a game that lets you sign in with Google Play Games may save your progress to your account (see section 22). If an App offers cloud storage, backup or synchronization, it will tell you, before you use that feature, what is stored, which provider stores it and how to delete it. Information stored this way is used only to provide that feature.

12. Advertising

Many of our Apps are free and supported by ads, shown through Google AdMob. Our Apps may use any of the ad formats that AdMob offers: banner ads, interstitial (full-screen) ads, rewarded and rewarded interstitial ads that give an in-game reward for watching, native ads that are shown as part of the App's own screens, and app open ads that appear when the App is opened or brought back to the foreground. The formats used vary from App to App, and the same categories of information described in this policy may be processed whichever format is shown. We do not currently use ad mediation (other ad networks through AdMob). Not every App shows ads.

To show and measure ads, AdMob may process the advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic information.

Personalized and non-personalized ads

Depending on your device settings, your location and how an App is configured, the ads you see may be personalized (based on information associated with your device's advertising ID) or non-personalized (based on more limited context, such as the App itself and your approximate location). Where the law requires your consent before personalized ads are shown or certain data is used, consent will be requested where required, and personalized ads are shown only if you give that consent. Ads may link to third-party websites or apps that are outside our control.

Consent

If you are in a region that requires it, the App shows a consent form the first time you open it, where you can choose whether to allow personalized ads and related data use. You can change your choice later from the App's settings.

Advertising is kept separate from sensitive information: we do not use health or other sensitive information described in section 8 for advertising.

13. Analytics and diagnostics

Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) helps us understand how players and users use an App so we can improve it. It may process the app instance ID, the advertising ID (where available), device and app information, approximate location, and usage events (for example levels started and completed, features used and ads shown).

Some of our Apps may use Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes and errors. It may collect crash logs and stack traces, your device model, operating system version, the state of the App and device at the time of a crash, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. We use this only to find and fix bugs.

15. How information is used

Depending on the App, information may be used to:

  • provide the App and the features you use, including records or content you create;
  • show advertising in Apps that have it, which helps keep those Apps free to use;
  • measure ad delivery and performance, and detect and prevent ad fraud and abuse;
  • understand how people use the App, for example which levels are played and where players stop;
  • detect, diagnose and fix crashes, errors and performance problems;
  • update app settings and features remotely, without requiring a new version of the App;
  • in Apps that include the features in section 14: send notifications, deliver in-app purchases, and save progress or show leaderboards and achievements;
  • maintain, improve and develop our Apps;
  • respond to messages you send us; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

Sensitive information is used only as described in section 8.

16. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared:

  • with Google, as the provider of AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config and the other features described in section 14, when an App uses those services;
  • when required by law, for example in response to a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety or property of users, Sk_Tech or others; and
  • in a business transfer, if an App is sold or transferred, in which case the new owner would be bound to handle information consistently with this policy or to notify you of changes.

In some places, showing personalized ads based on the advertising ID may be treated by law as "sharing" personal information or as "targeted advertising". You can opt out of personalized ads at any time; see section 26.

17. Data retention

Information collected by AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config and the other Google services described in section 14 is retained by Google for the periods described in Google's and Firebase's documentation, and in some cases according to retention settings available to us as the developer. See Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Information stored only on your device stays there until you delete it, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Information an App stores in the cloud (section 11) is kept until you delete it or ask us to, or for as long as the App tells you.

We keep emails you send us only for as long as needed to respond to you and to keep a reasonable record of our correspondence, unless a longer period is required by law. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

18. Deleting your data

  • On your device: use the App's own delete or reset option where it has one, clear the App's data in your device settings, or uninstall the App.
  • Cloud or account data: where an App stores information in the cloud or offers an account, it will provide a way to delete it, or you can email us to request deletion.
  • Information held by Google: email us and tell us which App your request is about. Where we cannot delete it ourselves, we will tell you and, where possible, explain how to make the request through Google's tools. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Emails you sent us: email us and we will delete them.

19. Getting a copy of your data

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you by emailing shani.ktk77@gmail.com. Where an App offers an export feature, you can also use it to get a copy of the records you created. Information that stays only on your device is not available to us, so we cannot provide a copy of it.

20. Security

We rely on the security measures of our service providers, and we take reasonable steps to protect information that we can access, such as limiting access to our developer accounts. However, no method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

21. Encryption

Google describes how it protects information processed by its services, including encryption, in its Firebase Privacy and Security documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

Information an App stores on your device is protected by your device's own security, such as its screen lock and the operating system keeping each app's data separate. It is not additionally encrypted by the App unless that App says so. We encourage you to use a screen lock on your device.

22. Accounts and sign-in

Our Apps use different account models. Many, including our games, work without an account. Some may let you sign in with a service such as Google Play Games. If you do, the App may receive a player ID and display name, which are used to save your progress and to show leaderboards or achievements. Sign-in is handled by the provider; we do not receive your password.

If an App requires or offers an account in the future, it will explain what information the account uses before you create it, and it will provide a way to request deletion of the account and its associated data.

23. Children's privacy

Our Apps may be used by people of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information such as their name, email address or phone number.

The third-party services described above may still process device identifiers, approximate location and usage information when a child uses an App. Where an App is directed to children, or where a player is known to be a child, our intention is to configure advertising and analytics in line with applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA in the United States and the GDPR in the European Union) and Google Play's Families policies, for example by requesting non-personalized, child-appropriate ads. How a specific App is configured may vary, and parents and guardians are encouraged to review an App's store listing and data safety section.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, or you want to ask about or request deletion of information related to your child, please contact us at shani.ktk77@gmail.com. We will respond and, where we are able, delete the information or explain how to request deletion from the relevant service provider.

24. International data processing

Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from yours. Google describes the safeguards it uses for these transfers in its own policies.

25. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or certain US states such as California), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information held about you, and receive a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, including processing for personalized advertising;
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, or of targeted advertising, where those terms apply;
  • withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent; and
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email shani.ktk77@gmail.com and tell us which App your request is about. Because many of our Apps do not ask for your name or email address, we may need information from you, such as an identifier shown in the App if one is available, to locate data related to your device. Much of the information described in this policy is held by Google; where we cannot act on a request ourselves, we will tell you and, where possible, explain how to make it through Google's tools. We will respond within the time required by applicable law, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

26. Your choices and controls

  • Ad personalization: on Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device). You can also manage ad settings at My Ad Center.
  • Consent choices: where an App shows a consent or privacy-options message, you can use it to make or change your choices. You can reopen it later from the App's settings.
  • Permissions: you can refuse or revoke an App's permissions in your device settings.
  • Notifications: if an App sends push notifications, you can turn them off at any time in your device settings.
  • Network access: using an App offline generally prevents these services from sending data until the device reconnects.
  • Stop all collection: you can stop all further collection by an App by uninstalling it.

This page remains the one policy for all of our Apps, at the same address. When we update it, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will take reasonable steps to let you know, such as through the App or its store listing. Please review this page periodically.
