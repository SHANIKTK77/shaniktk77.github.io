1. Who we are and scope

Sk_Tech is an independent game developer. This policy applies to the mobile games that Sk_Tech publishes (for example on Google Play) and that link to this page from their store listing or from within the game (each a "Game").

Not every Game uses every service described below. A specific Game may use some, all or none of them, depending on how that Game is built. Where a Game's store listing, data safety section or in-game notice gives more specific information about that Game, that information applies in addition to this policy.

This policy does not cover the websites, apps or services of third parties, including advertisers whose ads appear in our Games. Their own privacy policies apply when you interact with them.

2. Information we may process

Information you give us directly

Our Games are not designed to require you to create an account or to enter your name, email address or phone number in order to play. If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and anything you choose to include in your message, and we use it to respond to you.

Information collected automatically through third-party services

When a Game uses the services described in section 3, those services may automatically collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Device and app identifiers, such as the device's advertising ID (for example, the Android advertising ID), app set ID, Firebase installation ID and app instance ID.
  • Device and technical information, such as device model, operating system and version, language and region settings, screen size, app version, and network or carrier information.
  • Approximate location, which may be inferred from your IP address. Our Games do not request access to your device's precise (GPS) location.
  • Usage and gameplay information, such as when the Game is opened, sessions and their length, screens or levels visited, and in-game events.
  • Advertising information, such as ads shown, ad clicks and other interactions with ads.
  • Diagnostic and crash information, such as crash logs, stack traces, device state at the time of a crash (for example, available memory and disk space, orientation and whether the device is rooted) and performance data.
  • IP address, which is transmitted whenever a device connects to these services.

We do not sell this information, and we do not use these services to collect the content of your messages, contacts, photos or files.

3. Third-party services

The services below are provided by Google. When a Game uses them, Google processes information as described here and in Google's own policies. We do not control how Google processes this information.

Third-party services that our Games may use
Service What it does in our Games Information it may process
Google AdMob Shows banner advertisements. We do not currently use ad mediation (other ad networks through AdMob). Advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information.
Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) Helps us understand how players use the Game so we can improve it. App instance ID, advertising ID (where available), device and app information, approximate location, and usage and gameplay events.
Firebase Crashlytics Reports crashes and errors so we can find and fix them. Crash and error reports, stack traces, device state at the time of a crash, device and app information, and Crashlytics installation identifiers.

Advertising

Depending on your device settings, your location and how a Game is configured, the ads you see may be personalized (based on information associated with your device's advertising ID) or non-personalized (based on more limited context, such as the Game itself and your approximate location). Where the law requires your consent before personalized ads are shown or certain data is used, consent will be requested where required. Ads may link to third-party websites or apps that are outside our control.

4. How information is used

Information processed through these services may be used to:

  • show advertising in the Game, which helps keep our Games free to play;
  • measure ad delivery and performance, and detect and prevent ad fraud and abuse;
  • understand how players use the Game, for example which levels are played and where players stop;
  • detect, diagnose and fix crashes, errors and performance problems;
  • maintain, improve and develop our Games;
  • respond to messages you send us; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared:

  • with Google, as the provider of AdMob, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, when a Game uses those services (see section 3);
  • when required by law, for example in response to a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety or property of players, Sk_Tech or others; and
  • in a business transfer, if a Game is sold or transferred, in which case the new owner would be bound to handle information consistently with this policy or to notify you of changes.

6. Data retention

Information collected by AdMob, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics is retained by Google for the periods described in Google's and Firebase's documentation, and in some cases according to retention settings available to us as the developer. See Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy for details.

We keep emails you send us only for as long as needed to respond to you and to keep a reasonable record of our correspondence, unless a longer period is required by law. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

7. Security

We rely on the security measures of our service providers, and we take reasonable steps to protect information that we can access, such as limiting access to our developer accounts. However, no method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

  • Ad personalization: on Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device). You can also manage ad settings at My Ad Center.
  • Consent choices: where a Game shows a consent or privacy-options message, you can use it to make or change your choices.
  • Network access: playing a Game offline generally prevents these services from sending data until the device reconnects.
  • Stop all collection: you can stop all further collection by a Game by uninstalling it.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or certain US states), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information held about you, and receive a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, including processing for personalized advertising;
  • withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent; and
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email shani.ktk77@gmail.com and tell us which Game your request is about. Because our Games do not ask for your name or email address, we may need information from you, such as an identifier shown in the Game if one is available, to locate data related to your device. Much of the information described in this policy is held by Google; where we cannot act on a request ourselves, we will tell you and, where possible, explain how to make it through Google's tools. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Children's privacy

Our Games may be played by people of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information such as their name, email address or phone number.

The third-party services described above may still process device identifiers, approximate location and usage information when a child plays a Game. Where a Game is directed to children, or where a player is known to be a child, our intention is to configure advertising and analytics in line with applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA in the United States and the GDPR in the European Union) and Google Play's Families policies, for example by requesting non-personalized, child-appropriate ads. How a specific Game is configured may vary, and parents and guardians are encouraged to review a Game's store listing and data safety section.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, or you want to ask about or request deletion of information related to your child, please contact us at shani.ktk77@gmail.com. We will respond and, where we are able, delete the information or explain how to request deletion from the relevant service provider.

11. International transfers

Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from yours. Google describes the safeguards it uses for these transfers in its own policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when a Game starts or stops using a service. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will take reasonable steps to let you know, such as through the Game or its store listing. Please review this page periodically.
