1. Who we are and scope

Sk_Tech is an independent game developer. This single policy applies to all of the mobile apps and games that Sk_Tech publishes, now and in the future (for example on Google Play), and that link to this page from their store listing or from within the app (each an "App").

Not every App uses every service or feature described below. A specific App may use some, all or none of them, depending on how that App is built. Section 3 describes the services our Apps commonly use, and section 4 describes features that only some Apps may include. Where an App's store listing, data safety section or in-app notice gives more specific information about that App, that information applies in addition to this policy.

This policy does not cover the websites, apps or services of third parties, including advertisers whose ads appear in our Apps. Their own privacy policies apply when you interact with them.

2. Information we may process

Information you give us directly

Our Apps are not designed to require you to create an account or to enter your name, email address or phone number in order to play. If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and anything you choose to include in your message, and we use it to respond to you. Some Apps may offer a sign-in feature; see section 4.

Information stored on your device

Our Apps store your progress and settings (for example, levels completed and sound or language options) locally on your device so the App can pick up where you left off. This data stays on your device unless the App includes a sign-in feature that saves progress to your account (see section 4). You can remove it by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

Information collected automatically through third-party services

When an App uses the services described in section 3, those services may automatically collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Device and app identifiers, such as the device's advertising ID (for example, the Android advertising ID), app set ID, Firebase installation ID and app instance ID.
  • Device and technical information, such as device model, operating system and version, language and region settings, screen size, app version, and network or carrier information.
  • Approximate location, which may be inferred from your IP address. Our Apps do not request access to your device's precise (GPS) location.
  • Usage and gameplay information, such as when the App is opened, sessions and their length, screens or levels visited, and in-app events (for example levels started and completed, features used and ads shown).
  • Advertising information, such as ads shown, ad clicks and other interactions with ads.
  • Diagnostic and crash information, such as crash logs, stack traces, device state at the time of a crash (for example, available memory and disk space, orientation and whether the device is rooted) and performance data.
  • IP address, which is transmitted whenever a device connects to these services.

Apps that include one of the optional features in section 4 may process a few additional items, such as a notification token, purchase confirmations or a player ID. These are described in that section.

We do not sell this information, and we do not use these services to collect the content of your messages, contacts, photos or files.

Advertising

Our Apps may use any of the ad formats that AdMob offers: banner ads, interstitial (full-screen) ads, rewarded and rewarded interstitial ads that give an in-game reward for watching, native ads that are shown as part of the App's own screens, and app open ads that appear when the App is opened or brought back to the foreground. The formats used vary from App to App, and the same categories of information described in this policy may be processed whichever format is shown.

Depending on your device settings, your location and how an App is configured, the ads you see may be personalized (based on information associated with your device's advertising ID) or non-personalized (based on more limited context, such as the App itself and your approximate location). Where the law requires your consent before personalized ads are shown or certain data is used, consent will be requested where required, and personalized ads are shown only if you give that consent. Ads may link to third-party websites or apps that are outside our control.

Consent

If you are in a region that requires it, the App shows a consent form the first time you open it, where you can choose whether to allow personalized ads and related data use. You can change your choice later from the App's settings.

Firebase Remote Config

Our Apps may use Firebase Remote Config, a service provided by Google Firebase, to manage and update certain app settings and features remotely without requiring users to download a new version of the App.

To fetch these settings, Firebase Remote Config may process technical information, such as app and device-related information and a Firebase installation ID, as necessary to provide and maintain the service.

This information is processed in accordance with Google's and Firebase's applicable privacy practices. We do not intentionally use Remote Config to collect sensitive personal information.

For more information, please review the Firebase Privacy and Security information.

4. Features some of our apps may include

The features below apply only to Apps that include them. Each one is provided by Google, and Google's policies linked in section 3 apply to it as well.

Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

Some of our Apps may send push notifications. To do this, Firebase Cloud Messaging creates a notification token for your device, which is used to deliver reminders and updates about the App. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

Some of our Apps may use Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes and errors. It may collect crash logs and stack traces, your device model, operating system version, the state of the App and device at the time of a crash, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. We use this only to find and fix bugs.

In-app purchases

Some of our Apps may offer in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your card or payment details; we receive only the purchase confirmation and order information needed to deliver the item you bought.

In-app review and in-app update prompts

Some of our Apps may ask you to rate the App or to install a newer version without leaving the App. These prompts are provided by Google Play, and we do not collect any additional personal data through them.

Accounts and sign-in

Some of our Apps may let you sign in with a service such as Google Play Games. If you do, the App may receive a player ID and display name, which are used to save your progress and to show leaderboards or achievements.

5. How information is used

Information processed through these services may be used to:

  • show advertising in the App, which helps keep our Apps free to use;
  • measure ad delivery and performance, and detect and prevent ad fraud and abuse;
  • understand how players use the App, for example which levels are played and where players stop;
  • detect, diagnose and fix crashes, errors and performance problems;
  • update app settings and features remotely, without requiring a new version of the App;
  • in Apps that include the features in section 4: send notifications, deliver in-app purchases, and save progress or show leaderboards and achievements;
  • maintain, improve and develop our Apps;
  • respond to messages you send us; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared:

  • with Google, as the provider of AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config and the other features described in section 4, when an App uses those services (see sections 3 and 4);
  • when required by law, for example in response to a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety or property of players, Sk_Tech or others; and
  • in a business transfer, if an App is sold or transferred, in which case the new owner would be bound to handle information consistently with this policy or to notify you of changes.

7. Data retention

Information collected by AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config and the other Google services described in section 4 is retained by Google for the periods described in Google's and Firebase's documentation, and in some cases according to retention settings available to us as the developer. See Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy for details.

We keep emails you send us only for as long as needed to respond to you and to keep a reasonable record of our correspondence, unless a longer period is required by law. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

8. Security

We rely on the security measures of our service providers, and we take reasonable steps to protect information that we can access, such as limiting access to our developer accounts. However, no method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

  • Ad personalization: on Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device). You can also manage ad settings at My Ad Center.
  • Consent choices: where an App shows a consent or privacy-options message, you can use it to make or change your choices. You can reopen it later from the App's settings.
  • Notifications: if an App sends push notifications, you can turn them off at any time in your device settings.
  • Network access: using an App offline generally prevents these services from sending data until the device reconnects.
  • Stop all collection: you can stop all further collection by an App by uninstalling it.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or certain US states), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information held about you, and receive a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, including processing for personalized advertising;
  • withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent; and
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email shani.ktk77@gmail.com and tell us which App your request is about. Because our Apps do not ask for your name or email address, we may need information from you, such as an identifier shown in the App if one is available, to locate data related to your device. Much of the information described in this policy is held by Google; where we cannot act on a request ourselves, we will tell you and, where possible, explain how to make it through Google's tools. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

11. Children's privacy

Our Apps may be used by people of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information such as their name, email address or phone number.

The third-party services described above may still process device identifiers, approximate location and usage information when a child uses an App. Where an App is directed to children, or where a player is known to be a child, our intention is to configure advertising and analytics in line with applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA in the United States and the GDPR in the European Union) and Google Play's Families policies, for example by requesting non-personalized, child-appropriate ads. How a specific App is configured may vary, and parents and guardians are encouraged to review an App's store listing and data safety section.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, or you want to ask about or request deletion of information related to your child, please contact us at shani.ktk77@gmail.com. We will respond and, where we are able, delete the information or explain how to request deletion from the relevant service provider.
